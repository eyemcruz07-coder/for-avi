i made something small for you.

hi baby, i made this little space just for you.

avi, i just wanted to make this little something for you because sometimes i don't know how to express everything that i feel through words.

i know that not everything has been easy for us. we've had our disagreements, our times where we've been frustrated, tired, or unsure about how to convey our thoughts. but even after everything, i still find myself choosing you. i still want to learn about you and understand you.

thank you for all the small things that you do even if they may not mean much to you. thank you for everything that you do. the sudden laughs, the calls, the comfort and just being someone who i can spend my day with. honestly, you have made a lot of my normal moments more special because of your presence.

also, i would like you to remember that there is no need for you to be okay in front of me. you don't have to have everything figured out and the right words. if you are tired, shy, overwhelmed or simply having a tough day, that's fine too. it's okay if you need some time. i will be there for you and will always pray to understand you rather than forcing you to cope on your own.

i know that both of us are still learning about loving each other, and i do not expect that we will have everything figured out yet. i just hope that we continue to make the decision to communicate, be patient with one another, and come back to one another.

no matter how ordinary the day is, i'm always happy that somehow, i get to share a part of it with you.

i love you, avi. more than i can even explain sometimes. but i am very glad that it is you. ♡

aj ♡
